Buttons

One button, three choices: Style (how loud), Tone (what kind of action), Size. Primary and Secondary sit on a 3px ledge and drop into it when pressed. Press any of them.

Style × Tone

States

Size

In use

Full width at the bottom of a screen or sheet. One Primary per view.

Rules

Primary
The one action the screen exists for. Indigo fill, darker indigo border and ledge.
Secondary
A real alternative. Tinted fill, indigo border and ledge.
Tertiary
Dismiss, skip, learn more. Label only.
Destructive
Removes or cancels something the user would miss. Available in all three styles.
Neutral
Tertiary only. A quiet grey label for "not now".
Label
Large uses Label/XLarge/Medium (Phudu 18). Small uses Label/Medium/Medium (Phudu 14).
Icon
Optional trailing chevron for actions that move forward.
