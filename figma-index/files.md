# Fave in Figma: file index

Every file in the two folders that hold Fave's product design, in the **Fave India** team: **Fave 2026** (8 files) and **Fave Redesign 2024** (37 files). Scanned read-only on 8 Nov 2026.

Use this to answer "where is the design for X?" Every link opens the page or section in Figma. Pages whose names contain "ignore", "rough", "archive" or "old" were not scanned and aren't listed.

**Kinds of file**
- **Library**: holds reusable components and tokens.
- **Product**: screens for the Fave app.
- **Compliance**: screens made for certification (NPCI, BBPS).
- **Brand**: marketing, social, merch, website.
- **Exploration**: assignments, inspiration, prototypes.
- **Archive**: retired.

"Screens" counts frames about 393 wide and over 600 tall, so it's approximate and includes duplicates. "Unstyled text" is the share of text layers with no text style.

## Where to look first

- **How Fave should look now:** [non-group splits, DS applied](https://www.figma.com/design/HzRKVqMB9FKCLgEUw3W4G3?node-id=1917-10506), Settings Redesign, Biometric Payments (Fave DS section), Autopay Interoperability.
- **How it looked in 2024 (Onest era):** V0.1 to V0.7. Use these for flows and content, not for visual style.
- **Assets to bring into the library:** bank logos (V0.1), avatars (V0.7), bill category icons (Your money), navbar icons (Navbar Interactions), app icon and wordmark (V0.1, SplurgeKit).
