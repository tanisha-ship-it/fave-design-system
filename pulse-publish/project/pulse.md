## Playbook: what Zomato and Swiggy do, translated to money

- **Speed over polish.** They react in hours (a match, a monsoon, a rival's birthday). Fave's units of reaction are a push notification, the home hero card, an empty state line, a receipt footer, a split or group name suggestion. Build those as slots so a moment is a copy change, not a release.
- **One line, not a campaign.** Zomato's 2026 newspaper ad for Swiggy's birthday had no logo and one sentence; the brand colour was the joke. Fave's equivalents are Phudu uppercase on peach, the ledge, Money Uncle's dry line.
- **Self-aware, never smug.** Jokes land on the situation (the friend who never pays), never on the user, a community, a rival's customers or a tragedy.
- **The product is the punchline.** Zomato's moments end in an order; Fave's end in a settle, a split or a saved group. If the screen can't end there, it's content, not a wedge.
- **No cashback as personality.** Fave is finding PMF without cashback; a moment never becomes a discount.
